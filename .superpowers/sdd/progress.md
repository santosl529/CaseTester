## Rules in force
- Code corrects a computed figure only with the checker's agreement (3% / 0.5), naming the quantity; deterministic step, arithmetic and pricing flags only probe (13 Oct).
- No code-written correction or code probe unless the checker also flags that claim: a turn after its message at the earliest; rated correct or not rated → dropped, logged; the growth probe only of a flagged figure (Lorenzo, 16 Oct).
- New-case step flags and the ledger lookup stay report-only until zero false flags on fresh transcripts (`stepFlagsLive`, `ledgerLookupLive`; prof-001 step flags only).
- The four new cases run 25 minutes, budgets ×1.25 with 3 minutes for recommendation + risk + close (prof-002 since 13 Oct, the others 19 Oct); prof-001 20.
- Larkspur's brainstorm waits on Exhibit 3; Hollis Bay's Exhibit 3 waits on the brainstorm.
- The end gate reads the stage record only (one source of truth for what's owed).
- One error, one count; at most two probes per step per interview; after two, code's correction once or the report.
- Checker probes come in two steps and never lead; the hint is the second probe; no probe carries arithmetic.
- A checker line counts as delivered only if heard; code says it when the model leaves it out (15 Oct).
- The unit check uses the base the candidate states; a figure right on their own base is no flag (at most a period question); every code-written correction names its quantity and period; no bare "It's closer to X, not Y" (15 Oct).
- A late recommendation ask (the time warning, or code's ask past the recommendation deadline) also asks the biggest risk, in one question (15 Oct).
- A due stage question (past its deadline or the time warning; the risk once the recommendation is in) beats a checker probe: the probe is held to the next turn, once; a correction goes first; a held line may hold the close once, never at time-up (15 Oct).
- A concession withdraws only the claim it answers; a redo to a case-figure right answer only when it names the same quantity (15 Oct).
- Before the recommendation, only a conclusion-changing flag holds the close (15 Oct). From the recommendation reply on, every checker flag holds the close once per figure, and a final answer whose verdict hasn't landed holds it one turn (the stage's follow-up); the redo of code's probe holds nothing; only time-up overrides (16 Oct).
- A per-store transaction-volume or ticket ask gets the ticket, not revenue per store (16 Oct).
- Stage deadlines from the phase budgets: lead exhibit and core analysis at the analysis budget's end, ordered exhibits and the brainstorm at the exhibit budget's end, the recommendation ask at the brainstorm budget's end (15 Oct; all approved by Lorenzo, 15 Oct).
- The "margin problem, or normal growth?" probe only of dollar or cost-growth claims (15 Oct).
- No refusal of data the case holds (`factAnchors.covers`); a COGS split goes out whole; the release cap never splits a group.
- A recommendation the candidate gives counts as received; the risk question counts in any risk wording; a risk named with its mitigation in the candidate's own recommendation counts as the risk (Lorenzo, 16 Oct).
